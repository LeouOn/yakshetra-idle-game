## Scope of this gate

The Concept gate is the panel's first look at the **project's representation approach as a whole** — before any content is written. Reviewers assess whether the scope as bounded by `panel.md` is coherent and whether the disclaimer, prohibition lists, and lens framing are sufficient to bound the harm surface for the work that follows. No event scripts, endings, art, or mechanics are reviewed at this gate.

Per-gate reviewer remit (panel.md §3 applies to every gate; restated here for convenience):

- **R1** assesses historical plausibility and in-milieu coherence of the Tang Life 1 setting as described in the brief.
- **R2** assesses whether the framing as described could be read as a false doctrinal assertion.
- **R3** assesses whether the concept as described risks stereotyping or exoticization of living Chinese / Chinese-diaspora communities.
- **R4** assesses whether the concept as described would read as respectful to a practicing lay household.

Explicitly **out of scope** for every reviewer (panel.md §3): game-design feel, monetization, technical architecture, Theravāda/Vajrayāna content, and doctrinal correction of non-religious design choices.
